*(June 14, 2026)* — nmTeam officially marks its fifth anniversary today.

Five years ago, nmTeam started with a simple idea: to create products and services that are truly useful, fun, and unmistakably “nm”. Five years later, we have gradually built a service ecosystem centered around nmBot, covering group management, intelligent interaction, account systems, support documentation, and more.

Over the past year, nmTeam’s focus has gradually shifted from improving individual features to building and integrating overall product capabilities.

As the core product in our ecosystem, nmBot continued to evolve throughout the year. Around the direction of “nm + AI”, nmBot Intelligence has gradually become an important foundation connecting various product experiences. nmBot Copilot allows users to complete group management and personal configuration through more natural language. Anti-Spam 2.0 uses intelligent recognition capabilities to provide groups with more reliable protection against spam and harassment. The new Proper Warm Up and nmartChat+ also make nmBot’s performance in group interactions feel more natural and better aligned with real communication scenarios.

For developers and AI enthusiasts, we launched nmBot MCP and nmBot Agent Skills. These capabilities further open up nmBot’s operation interfaces, configuration experience, and product capabilities, allowing users to integrate nmBot into more flexible agent workflows. For us, this means more than opening up features; it also means that nmBot can participate in a wider range of scenarios. We hope it can be more than a bot product and become an important part of group management automation.

Over the past year, nmBot+ continued to be optimized for professional users and large groups. We continued to update and improve features such as leave messages, automatic deletion of group operation reply messages, administrator call message receiving settings, semi-automatic join verification, custom anti-spam rules, and emotion recognition for keyword replies. These updates allow groups to gain richer management capabilities with lower administrative costs. At the same time, nmBot+ continues to maintain a low entry barrier and strong value for money, enabling groups of different sizes to use advanced features according to their own needs.

This year, the nmBot Panel also completed a new round of visual and experience upgrades. We continued to optimize the interface structure, device adaptation, configuration flow, and feature entry points, aiming to make complex group management operations more intuitive. Meanwhile, features such as scheduled tasks, automatic message deletion, channel identity control, emergency mode, joint prevention and control mechanisms, and commercial scheduled mode were further improved, making nmBot more stable and reliable in real-world usage scenarios.

As of this year, nmBot has served tens of thousands of groups and channels and has more than 570,000 active users. This number is encouraging to us, and it also reminds us of the responsibility we carry. Every feature update, experience adjustment, and service maintenance effort ultimately affects the real experience of real users. We remain cautious toward this trust and are willing to invest more time into continuously refining our products.

Beyond nmBot, nmTeam is also constantly improving the foundational services that support the entire ecosystem. Over the past year, platforms such as the nmTeam Account Center and Support website have continued to iterate, taking on responsibilities including account management, security protection, help documentation, information publishing, and user communication. The development of a product ecosystem depends on feature innovation, as well as stable infrastructure, a complete documentation system, and transparent communication mechanisms.

Accessibility remains an important long-term area of investment for nmTeam. Across multiple projects, we continue to focus on semantic structure, content readability, keyboard operation experience, and compatibility with assistive technologies, while integrating related improvements into our daily development process. We hope every user can smoothly access and use nmTeam’s products and services. Accessibility should not remain only at the level of principle; it should be reflected in every design, development, and release process.

Looking ahead, nmTeam will continue to improve nmBot Intelligence, nmBot+, group management automation, developer capability openness, product accessibility, and foundational service experience. We will also continue to listen to user feedback, discover problems in real usage scenarios, and improve our products through long-term maintenance, making them more stable, easier to use, and more trustworthy.

We would like to thank all users, group administrators, channel operators, developers, friends, and supporters for accompanying us over the past five years. It is these real usage experiences, feedback, and suggestions that have pushed nmTeam forward step by step and brought that small original idea to where it is today.
