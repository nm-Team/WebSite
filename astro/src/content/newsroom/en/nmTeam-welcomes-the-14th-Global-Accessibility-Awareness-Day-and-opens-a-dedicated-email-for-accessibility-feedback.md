*(May 15, 2025)* — Today marks the 14th Global Accessibility Awareness Day (GAAD). Every year on this occasion, nmTeam reflects on and shares our efforts and progress in product accessibility over the past year. We firmly believe that the power of technology lies in empowering every individual; regardless of their physical condition, everyone should have equal rights to access information and use services.

In the past year, we have continued to fulfill our commitment to accessible experiences. Our newly launched nmTeam Account Center and nmTeam Support website in the past year, as well as the interface updates to nmTeam Newsroom and nmBot Panel, were all designed with accessibility as a priority from the outset. This ensures these new platforms and services fully considered the needs of all types of users from the very beginning, allowing every user, including those with disabilities, to access and use them with ease.

Meanwhile, we have not stopped optimizing our existing products. Over the past year, we have made several accessibility improvements to the nmTeam official website and the nmBot panel, such as optimizing screen reader compatibility, enhancing the reliability of keyboard navigation, and improving content readability, all aimed at enhancing their usability and inclusivity.

![nmTeam's official website adds the "Skip to Content" button to help users start browsing faster. ](/newsroom/uploads/2025/05/4168053721.png)

Looking ahead, we understand that the journey towards accessibility is endless. We plan to introduce more in-depth accessibility improvements for the nmBot panel in the near future to further enhance the user experience in complex operational scenarios.

To better listen to our users' voices and continuously improve the accessibility of our products, we are officially announcing today the launch of a dedicated accessibility feedback email address: accessibility@nmteam.xyz.

If you encounter any accessibility issues while using nmTeam products and services, or if you have any valuable opinions or suggestions, please feel free to contact us via this email. Every piece of your feedback is a driving force for our progress, helping us build more inclusive digital products.

nmTeam will continue to be committed to promoting technological inclusion and will work tirelessly to build a digital world that everyone can access with ease. You can learn more about our commitment to accessibility at [https://nmteam.xyz/products/overview/product-accessibility](https://nmteam.xyz/products/overview/product-accessibility).
