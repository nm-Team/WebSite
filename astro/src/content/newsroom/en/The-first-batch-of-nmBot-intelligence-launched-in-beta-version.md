*(April 17, 2025)* — The first batch of [nmBot Intelligence](/en/newsroom/nmTeam-releases-nmBot-Intelligence-2025-04-01/) features, “nmBot Intelligence Chat (Beta)”, is now available. Users can join the waitlist via [link](https://t.me/nmnmfunbot?start=intelligencewaitlist) starting today.

![](/newsroom/uploads/2025/03/2421807792.png)

"**nmBot Intelligence Chat**" is a built-in personal assistant within nmBot and nmBot Panel, making nmBot group management and personal settings unprecedentedly simple.

With "nmBot Intelligence Chat", you only need to describe your goals in natural language, and nmBot Intelligence will configure the corresponding functions for you.

For example, simply say "Require new members to subscribe to my channel @nmbotchannel and restrict image posting on their first day", nmBot Intelligence will enable **Join Verification** and **New Member Restriction** features automatically. Say "Block image/video posts between 11 PM to 8 AM", nmBot Intelligence will create corresponding scheduled tasks.

You can also ask "nmBot Intelligence Chat" about how to use various nmBot features. Later this year, "nmBot Intelligence Chat" will integrate with nmBot documentation to provide enhanced assistance.

Available both in Telegram bot interface and nmBot Panel, "nmBot Intelligence Chat" deeply integrates with all nmBot features to deliver a smoother user experience.

"nmBot Intelligence Chat (Beta)" will be gradually available to users. Starting today, users can join the "nmBot Intelligence Chat (Beta)" waiting list through the link https://t.me/nmnmfunbot?start=intelligencewaitlist. Once "nmBot Intelligence Chat (Beta)" is available to users, nmBot will send a notification to users.

In addition to nmBot intelligence, this month's nmBot feature update also brings functions such as group configuration import and export. You can [learn about the complete changes in the nmBot help document](https://nmbotdoc.nmnm.fun/update-log/2025-04/).

What's more, the [nmBot product introduction page](https://nmteam.xyz/products/overview/nmBot-Telegram) has also been updated to introduce various nmBot intelligence features.
