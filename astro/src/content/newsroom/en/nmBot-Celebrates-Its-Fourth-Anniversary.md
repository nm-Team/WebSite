*(April 27, 2026)* — nmBot, the Telegram group management bot under nmTeam, officially marks its fourth anniversary since launch. Over the past four years, nmBot has consistently been built around user needs, earning the trust and affection of group administrators and everyday users through continuous technological innovation and service improvements.

Over the past year, our “nm + AI” strategy has continued to gain momentum. nmBot Intelligence is gradually becoming the core capability that connects the entire product experience. Our newly introduced **nmBot Copilot** allows users to apply nmBot’s powerful features in a way that feels closer to everyday language. With the support of nmBot Intelligence, **Harassment Interception 2.0** has improved its ability to identify abusive content by five times. The new **Proper Warm Up** and **nmartChat+** features also make group interactions more natural, more flexible, and more context-aware.

For professional users, we launched **nmBot MCP** and **nmBot Agent Skills** this month, making nmBot’s operational capabilities and our first-hand product experience fully available to developers and AI enthusiasts. With our MCP and skills, users can easily give “AI lobster” the ability to configure nmBot, enabling a higher level of intelligence and automation.

Over the past year, **nmBot+** has continued to strengthen its value for professional users and large groups. We have added a range of new features, including leave messages, automatic deletion of group operation reply messages, callout message receiving settings for group administrators, support in harassment interception custom rules for sticker pack names and emoji status sticker pack names, manual emergency mode termination, semi-automatic join verification, and emotion recognition for keyword replies. At the same time, we have continued to offer nmBot+ at an affordable price with excellent value for money. Combined with flexible tiered pricing and trial plans for large groups, nmBot+ continues to bring more complete and more professional group management capabilities to more communities.

This year, nmBot has shifted from rapid growth to high-quality growth. We have fully redesigned the nmBot panel to make it more modern, more visually appealing, and better adapted to users’ devices. Existing features have also been upgraded across the board. For example, scheduled tasks can now be automatically pinned and deleted, and a variety of new auto-delete message types and channel identity control options make message policies more targeted. New features further improve the overall nmBot experience. Emergency mode helps groups respond to sudden incidents with greater composure, the joint prevention and control mechanism makes multi-group management easier, and business scheduled mode allows users to customize account information with ease.

nmBot continues to be warmly received by users. It now has more than **570,000 active users** and serves **tens of thousands of groups and channels**.

We would like to thank all users for their support, feedback, and companionship over the past four years. It is the real-world usage and suggestions from our users that have helped nmBot grow from a continuously evolving product into a more mature and reliable group and channel management tool. Looking ahead, nmTeam will continue working with our users to make nmBot even more stable, more powerful, and more convenient.
