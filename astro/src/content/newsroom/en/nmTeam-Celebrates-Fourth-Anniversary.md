*(June 14, 2025)* — Today, nmTeam proudly marks its fourth-anniversary milestone. We sincerely thank our global users and communities for their long-term trust and support. It is this companionship that drives us to continuously explore and move forward.

**nmBot's Technological Innovations Drive New Feature Development**

As the flagship product of the nmTeam ecosystem, nmBot has undergone a comprehensive evolution driven by technological innovation over the past year. Launched on nmTeam's third anniversary last year, nmBot NEXT introduced a brand-new modular unified architecture and deeply integrated nm+AI capabilities, bringing unprecedented intelligence and efficiency to nmBot. This revolutionary architecture enables it to support over 360,000 monthly active users and has increased core operational efficiency by up to 80%. Meanwhile, the upgraded nmBot panel offers a more intuitive and convenient smart management interface, which, combined with a high-performance message processing engine, significantly optimizes management workflows.

To meet the advanced management needs of professional users and large groups, we launched the nmBot+ value-added service. Leveraging the evolved permission controls and modular system of nmBot NEXT, it offers a series of powerful exclusive features, such as nmBot configuration permission management, custom spam rules, setting up group command lists, and new member permission restrictions, while comprehensively upgrading over a dozen core functions like join verification, scheduled tasks, and keyword replies—with features that continue to evolve.

nmBot+ adheres to a low-cost, accessible strategy, allowing about 83% of active groups to enjoy professional-grade services for an average cost of just $0.4 per month. Through flexible tiered pricing, custom plans, and a free trial for large groups, it ensures the high accessibility of the service.

**nmBot Intelligence Innovates Bot Interaction**

We believe that artificial intelligence can make operations simpler. The nmBot Intelligence feature set, introduced this April, embodies this philosophy. As a built-in assistant in both nmBot and its panel, it allows users to describe their needs in natural language to easily modify group and personal configurations, completely changing the complexity of traditional interactions. This innovation significantly lowers the barrier to configuring advanced features, making it easy for every user to harness the full power of nmBot.

nmBot Intelligence also empowers the "Proper Warm Up" feature, making nmBot's interactions within groups feel more natural. In the future, nmBot Intelligence will also serve as the core engine for upcoming features planned for later this year, such as nmartChat+ and Anti-Spam 2.0, providing more precise and natural intelligent-driven capabilities.

**Launching the nmTeam Account Center and Support Website**

A mature ecosystem relies on a unified, secure, and easily accessible service foundation. Last year, we officially launched the nmTeam Account Center, providing users with a seamless experience to log in to all nmTeam services with a single account. The new Account Center features a modernized design and introduces advanced security features like Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and Passkeys to fully protect user accounts and data. It also supports multiple languages and is committed to accessibility, ensuring all users can equally enjoy the convenience brought by technology.

To provide more comprehensive support, we also launched the new nmTeam Support website. This site integrates a wide range of resources, including nmBot user guides and Account Center help, offering users a one-stop hub for extensive documentation and solutions. More importantly, the support site is fully open-source on GitHub. We welcome users worldwide to participate in writing and improving the documentation, building an accurate and comprehensive knowledge base through an open collaboration model to ensure every user can get help promptly.

The fourth anniversary is a time for reflection, but more importantly, a look to the future. Standing at a new starting point, nmTeam will continue to focus on technology, listen to user feedback, and work with our global users to welcome a new era of more intelligent, convenient, and secure digital services.
