*(April 27, 2025)* — nmBot, the Telegram group management bot under nmTeam, officially celebrates its third anniversary today. Over the past three years, nmBot has consistently prioritized user needs, earning deep trust and affection from a wide range of users through continuous technological innovation and service optimization.

Looking back over the past three years, from its initial concept to becoming a powerful and well-regarded intelligent management platform, every step of nmBot's journey is a testament to the team's dedication and hard work, and couldn't have been achieved without the support and invaluable feedback from all users. nmBot deeply understands the challenges faced by community administrators and continuously evolves its product, striving to offer the most practical and efficient smart management tools to simplify and ease the often-tedious task of group management.

![Slide titled 'nmBot Widely Loved' showing three circle charts with statistics: 87% Satisfied with nmBot, 87% Willing to recommend nmBot to other group managers, and 74% Believe nmBot has improved group management efficiency. ](/newsroom/uploads/2025/04/2387711046.png)

nmBot has seen continuous growth in its user base and influence over the past three years. The latest user satisfaction survey results strongly validate nmBot's value: a high 87% of users report being satisfied with nmBot, demonstrating its excellence in product performance and user experience. Furthermore, 87% of users readily state their willingness to recommend nmBot to other group administrators, serving as strong evidence of its reputation and acceptance. Notably, 74% of users feel that nmBot has significantly boosted their group management efficiency, saving them substantial time and effort. Widely appreciated, nmBot has become an indispensable tool for many communities.

![Slide titled 'nmBot NEXT' highlighting key aspects: Security and Privacy, Brand New Modular Unified Architecture, nm+AI, 350K+ MAU, nmBot Intelligence logo, nmBot NEXT logo, nmBot+ logo, 80% Faster Operations, a screenshot of the nmBot Panel interface, and Efficient Message Handling. ](/newsroom/uploads/2025/04/2355263837.png)

In June 2024, aligning with nmTeam's third anniversary, nmBot introduced the revolutionary [nmBot NEXT](/en/newsroom/nmBot-NEXT-Officially-Launched-2024-06-14/). This new version features a completely redesigned modular unified architecture and deeply integrates nm+AI capabilities, bringing unprecedented intelligence and efficiency to nmBot. The architecture of nmBot NEXT enables it to support over 350,000 monthly active users, boosting core operation efficiency by up to 80%. The new nmBot  Panel provides a more intuitive and user-friendly smart management interface, which, combined with its high-performance message processing engine, significantly streamlines management workflows. Furthermore, nmBot NEXT offers industry-leading security and privacy protection, ensuring strong safeguarding of user data.

![Slide titled 'nmBot Intelligence' showing four feature cards: Intelligence Chat (Beta Available), PWU 3.0 (Available May 2025), nmartChat+ (Coming Later This Year), and Anti-Spam 2.0 (Coming Later This Year). ](/newsroom/uploads/2025/04/1615119449.png)

This month, nmBot launched [nmBot Intelligence](/en/newsroom/nmTeam-releases-nmBot-Intelligence-2025-04-01/), a collection of intelligent assistant features built directly into nmBot and nmBot panel. [Currently in Beta](/en/newsroom/The-first-batch-of-nmBot-intelligence-launched-in-beta-version-2025-04-17/), nmBot Intelligence Chat serves as a personal assistant, enabling users to easily manage groups and personalize settings by simply describing their needs in natural language. Features like Proper Warm Up 3.0, nmartChat+, and enhanced Anti-Spamwill also leverage nmBot intelligence for more accurate detection and natural interactions. The integration of these intelligence capabilities substantially lowers the setup threshold and elevates the sophistication of automated management.

![Slide titled 'nmBot+ Exclusive Group Benefits' listing features in two columns: Exclusive New Features (including Configuration Permissions, Custom Spam Rules, Custom Command List, Ban and Delete Recent Messages, etc.) and Newly Upgraded Features (including Mention Group Admins, Group Join Verification, Ban Duration for Leaving Users, Scheduled Tasks, etc.). ](/newsroom/uploads/2025/04/1514699575.png)

For advanced users and larger groups requiring more sophisticated capabilities, nmBot offers the [nmBot+](/en/newsroom/nmTeam-launches-nmBot-plus-2024-11-17/) service. This service provides a suite of exclusive group management permissions and enhanced features. nmBot+ subscribers gain access to premium functions such as nmBot Configuration Permission Management, Custom Spam Rules, the ability to customize Group Lommand List, Ban Users and Delete Recent Messages, Join Request Notifications, New Member Permission Restrictions, Warn Users by Message Type, and more. Robust tools including Join Verification, Customizable Ban Durations for Leaving Users, Scheduled Tasks, Keyword Replies, Welcome Messages, and unlock restrictions further empower automated management. Powered by nmBot Intelligence, these features become even more potent and intuitive, supporting the creation of higher-quality and better-organized community spaces.

![Slide titled 'nmBot+ Low Price Strategy' showing four sections: 83% of Groups Cost Only $0.4 per Month (with pie chart), Tiered Pricing Customized Plans (with stairs icon), Free Trial For Large Groups (with shopping bag icon), and Multiple Plans Continuous Monthly and Different Durations (with receipt icon).](/newsroom/uploads/2025/04/1905317194.png)

nmBot+ follows a low-pricing strategy designed to make professional-grade group management accessible to more users. For about 83% of active groups, subscriptions average just $0.4 per month. To accommodate groups of varying sizes and requirements, nmBot+ offers flexible tiered pricing and customized plans, including special free trial options for large groups. A variety of continuous monthly and different-length subscription options are also available, providing users with greater flexibility and ensuring high accessibility of the service.

As we stand at the new starting point of our third anniversary, the nmBot team remains committed to its mission of "making group management simpler." We will continue to innovate our products and services, with a particular focus on increasing investment in AI and smart technologies. Our goal is to be the most reliable partner for community administrators worldwide, as we step together with our users into a new era of intelligent community management.
